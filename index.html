Haulway – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Haulway ("the game") is made by Stephen Ndegwa. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The game contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data, and no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not need the internet to play.
  • Your progress (money, trucks, upgrades, hired drivers, the job in progress and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • The game requests no special permissions.

The game does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.
